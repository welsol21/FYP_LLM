You are building target notes for an English grammar teaching dataset.

Your task is NOT to explain one specific sentence.
Your task is to generate a reusable bank of pedagogical note targets for one grammar construction.

Use the fields below as input:
- GRAMMAR_PATTERN: the broad grammar family
- GRAMMAR_SUBTYPE: the narrower subtype, if available
- REFERENCE_NOTE: one trusted seed note
- EXAMPLE_SENTENCE: one example sentence for weak grounding only

Rules:
- Produce 15 candidate notes.
- Each note must be a single sentence.
- Each note must be reusable across many sentences of the same construction.
- Keep each note short and pedagogical.
- Prefer 8 to 18 words when possible.
- Do not make the notes depend on the exact vocabulary of the example sentence.
- Do not mention the example sentence directly unless absolutely necessary.
- Do not generate textbook-like paragraphs.
- Do not generate rule numbers, chapter references, CEFR labels, or source names.
- Do not use placeholders.
- Do not generate empty generic notes like "This sentence shows this grammar."
- Do not produce near-duplicates that differ only by one or two words.
- Each note must add a genuinely useful pedagogical angle.

Coverage requirements:
- At least 5 notes should focus on form or structure.
- At least 5 notes should focus on function, meaning, or discourse role.
- At least 5 notes should focus on usage, distribution, constraints, or contrasts.

Quality constraints:
- Notes must remain valid for the grammar pattern, not just the example.
- Notes must not contradict each other.
- Notes must not drift into unrelated grammar topics.
- If a candidate is too example-specific, do not include it.
- If two candidates are too similar, keep only the stronger one.

Output format:
- Return only the 15 notes.
- One note per line.
- No numbering.
- No bullets.
- No extra commentary.

Input:
GRAMMAR_PATTERN: {{GRAMMAR_PATTERN}}
GRAMMAR_SUBTYPE: {{GRAMMAR_SUBTYPE}}
REFERENCE_NOTE: {{REFERENCE_NOTE}}
EXAMPLE_SENTENCE: {{EXAMPLE_SENTENCE}}

Now generate 15 high-quality alternative pedagogical notes.
